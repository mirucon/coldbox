=== Accessibility Statement ===

Coldbox aims to keep its navigation, content and controls usable with a keyboard
and assistive technology. Site content, plugins and custom colors also affect
accessibility; the theme alone cannot establish a site's WCAG conformance.

== Quick Information ==

Accessibility audit completed: No

The current release has automated and keyboard regression checks. These are not
a complete WCAG audit or a new approval by the WordPress accessibility review team.

== Testing Tools and Methodology ==

Playwright checks rendered WordPress pages in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit.
axe-core checks representative pages and open search/menu states. Keyboard checks
cover skip links, search focus and dismissal, mobile navigation and comment tabs.
Additional checks cover narrow layouts and increased text spacing.
Browser accessibility trees are inspected; this is not testing by everyday
screen-reader users. Author-supplied content and arbitrary custom colors are not
certified by these checks.
